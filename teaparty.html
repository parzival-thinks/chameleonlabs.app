Teaparty

Keep your friends up to date without the work.

Teaparty reads the sources you connect, such as your messages, journal, calendar, and sent email, and writes short updates about your life in your own voice. You review every draft. Nothing goes out until you send it, and only to the friends you choose.

How it works

  • Connect what you like. Each source has its own consent, and you can turn any of them off.
  • Teaparty writes drafts. It can pick photos that fit, if you let it.
  • You edit, remove, or send. Your friends read your updates in the app, and can react and write back.

Teaparty is in private beta on iPhone and Mac.
