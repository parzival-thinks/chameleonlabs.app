FAQs

What is Kaching?

Kaching is an app to connect companies who want your opinions with people who they want opinions from, a.k.a. you! To compensate you for your opinions, we pay out cash rewards to people as they complete surveys - which can then be transferred to Paypal, CashApp, or Venmo.

What is the Bright Data SDK?

You will get access to surveys in exchange for safely using some of your device’s resources, and only in a manner that will not substantially affect the device's operation. You may turn this off from the settings menu. Please see our TOS [https://www.chameleonlabs.app/terms-of-service] and the SDK Privacy Policy at https://brightdata.com/legal/sdk-privacy for further information.
