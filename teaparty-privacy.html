Teaparty Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Teaparty is run by Chameleon Labs, LLC (“we”). This policy covers the Teaparty apps for iPhone and Mac and the service behind them. It doesn’t cover our other products.

What we collect

From you, directly

  • Your phone number, which you sign in with.
  • Your name, username, and profile picture.
  • Your friends, your posts, the photos and videos you attach, your reactions, and your messages in Inbox.
  • What you tell tea, the assistant in the app.

From the sources you connect

Each one is off until you agree to it.

  • Messages (Mac). The Mac app reads your iMessage history and uploads it to our server to write your drafts. This includes what other people wrote to you.
  • Contacts. Your address book’s names, phone numbers, and emails, to find friends who are on Teaparty and to recognize who you talk to.
  • Journal. Pictures of your journal pages are read once, then thrown away. We keep the text.
  • Google Calendar. Past events on your primary calendar.
  • Gmail. Only the email threads you’ve sent a message in. Nothing else in your mailbox is read, and no attachments.
  • Photos. If you turn this on, small copies of photos and videos from the days your drafts are about are used to pick the ones that fit. Only photos attached to a draft are uploaded and kept.

From your device

A push notification token, so we can tell you when something arrives.

How we use it

To write your drafts, pick photos, answer you in tea, show your posts to the friends you chose, deliver notifications, keep the service secure, and fix problems. We don’t show ads and we don’t sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • Friends. Only what you choose to send, to the people you choose.
  • Anthropic, our AI provider. The text, images, and photo copies needed for a task are sent to Anthropic’s API, which processes them to produce the result. Anthropic may keep them for up to 30 days, or longer if they’re flagged for breaking its usage policy or the law requires it, and doesn’t use them to train its models.
  • Google Cloud, which hosts the service and stores its data.
  • Google Firebase, which sends your sign-in code.
  • Apple, which delivers notifications.
  • Others, if the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety or our rights.

Google user data

If you connect Google, we read only what’s described above, using Google’s read-only Calendar and Gmail permissions, plus your Google address to show which account is connected.

We use this data only to provide the features you can see in Teaparty: writing your drafts and showing where a draft came from. We send it to Anthropic’s API for that purpose, as described above. We don’t sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train general AI models, and people don’t read it unless you ask us to, it’s needed for security, or the law requires it.

Teaparty’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

  • Gmail text: 30 days after we read it.
  • Conversations with tea: 30 days.
  • Journal pictures: not kept.
  • Other sources, posts, and account data: until you remove them, disconnect the source, or delete your account.

If you disconnect Google, we stop reading it and delete what we read. Excerpts already in your drafts stay until you delete those drafts. You can also remove Teaparty’s access in your Google Account.

Security

Your private data is encrypted on our servers with a key that’s yours alone, and only our service can use that key.

Deleting your account

Email support@chameleonlabs.app, and we may ask you to confirm the account is yours. We then delete your live data and revoke our Google access. Backups are removed on a rolling basis, within about 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off any source, remove any draft, and ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. Depending on where you live, including California and the EU, you may have further rights. Write to us and we’ll respond.

Other people’s information

Your messages and mail contain words from people who don’t use Teaparty. We use them only to write your own drafts, and you check every draft before it’s sent.

Children

Teaparty isn’t for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new date, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
