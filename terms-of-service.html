NOTE: Dreamshot uses a Neural Network Model Stable Diffusion that allows users to generate personalized photos. For all the important questions about how Stable Diffusion technology works, you can find answers in their FAQ.

How does it work?

  • Users upload 10 to 20 photos and select their gender and age.
  • Using these data, a copy of the Stable Diffusion model is retrained to personalize the model.
  • After the model copy is retrained, users can upload photos to personalize them
  • Immediately after the successful generation of your model, your original photos are deleted from our servers. We store your original photo on our servers for no longer than 24 hours. After 24 hours your original photos are deleted. Face data is never shared with 3rd parties.
  • We do not use your Personal Data to generally train and/or create our separate artificial intelligence/products.

We do our best to moderate the settings of the AI model, however, it is still possible that you may encounter content that you may see as inappropriate for you. Please contact us at chameleontower@gmail.com if you find any of the content to be offensive or inappropriate to you, we will promptly take the action and it will help us to train the algorithm better.
